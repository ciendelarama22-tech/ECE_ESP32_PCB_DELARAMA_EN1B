# ESP32 Expansion Board

This is a compact and efficient breakout board designed to unlock the full potential of the ESP32 DevKit V1. I built everything using **EasyEDA**—from the initial circuit schematic to the final PCB layout and 3D mockup.

---

## 🔥 Features
* **Perfect Fit:** Made specifically for the ESP32 DevKit V1.
* **Easy Wiring:** Breaks out all the GPIO pins so you don't have to struggle with messy breadboards.
* **Dual Power:** Built-in 3.3V and 5V power rails for your sensors.
* **Clean Power:** Uses an AMS1117 regulator to keep things stable.
* **Compact Layout:** Small footprint with a clean, easy-to-solder design.

---

## 🛠️ What's On The Board?

### The Brain
* **ESP32 DevKit V1** 

### Power Setup
* **DC Power Jack** (For external power)
* **AMS1117-3.3V** Voltage Regulator 
* **Slide Switch** (Main power switch)
* **100µF & 0.1µF Capacitors** (To filter out power noise)
* **LED + 1kΩ Resistor** (Power indicator light)

### Headers & Pins
* **1x15 & 1x15 Female Headers** (Where the ESP32 plugs in)
* **Male Pin Headers** (For easy jumper wire connections to GPIO, 3.3V, 5V, and GND)
